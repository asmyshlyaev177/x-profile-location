See which country any X account posts from, without opening a menu.

Hover a profile and the flag is there. Switch it on for the feed and it sits next to every handle.


WHAT YOU GET

- Country flag in hover cards, on profiles, and inline in the timeline
- A VPN badge when X itself cannot verify the location
- Collapse or hide posts from countries and regions you pick
- Highlight by keyword, or by flags stacked in a bio
- Swipe right to reveal, on Android
- No rate limit errors halfway down a long thread


X RATE LIMITS, SOLVED INSTEAD OF HIT

You have seen the failure. Flags fill in at the top of a thread, then stop. Or every profile you hover spins and never lands. That is the rate limit: X cuts you off at 50 account lookups per 15 minutes, and one busy thread has more accounts in it than that.

Most profiles here never cost a lookup, because they are already cached in your browser or answered by the shared cache. The rest is rationed against the live limit, and the last 30 percent is held back for accounts you actually hover.

Run it dry and you get a countdown to the reset, not a blank flag.


WHERE THE COUNTRY COMES FROM

Your browser asks x.com, using the session you are already logged into. No server of ours in the middle, no IP geolocation, no third party database.

So it is exactly as accurate as X's own data. Sometimes marked unverified, sometimes missing entirely on older accounts. No login and no API key.


FILTERING

Pick countries or whole regions, then leave them alone, collapse them behind a "Show" button, or hide them. Collapse is the default, because a timeline that silently deletes things is not one you can trust.


PRIVACY

Never read: your cookies and session tokens, the bios and posts you scroll past, your history. Not as a setting. It never reads them.

No analytics or telemetry in the extension. Results sit in your browser for 30 days and one button clears them.


THE SHARED CACHE, ON BY DEFAULT

Your lookups answer other people's and theirs answer yours, which is what keeps the rate limit off your back.

What gets sent: the handle looked up, its country and VPN flag, and a random install ID. Nothing about you, nothing about what you read. One toggle in options turns it off.


PERMISSIONS

x.com and twitter.com, because that is where it runs. Storage, for settings and the cache. Context menus, for a single "Options" entry.

That is the whole list. It cannot see any other tab you have open, and there is no remote code.


WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a bot or engagement-farming detector. It surfaces one signal you cannot otherwise see without opening every profile one at a time. What you conclude from it is yours.


Chrome, Edge and Brave on the desktop. Lemur Browser on Android.
